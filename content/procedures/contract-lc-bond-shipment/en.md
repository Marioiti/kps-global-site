The order is fixed by arithmetic. A performance bond is a percentage of the letter of credit, so a demand to issue the bond first cannot be met: the instrument it is calculated on does not exist yet. UCP 600 governs letters of credit; it does not govern guarantees, so the bond's terms are agreed in the contract.
