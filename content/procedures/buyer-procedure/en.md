steps:
  - title: "Send a current request"
    actor: "Buyer"
    document: "ICPO or letter of intent, in force and addressed to the right company"
    receives: "The seller receives a request it can answer with an offer."
  - title: "Receive and check the offer"
    actor: "Seller, then us"
    document: "Offer and corporate documents, sent to us first for checking"
    receives: "The buyer receives a checked offer, with its validity in banking days marked."
  - title: "Verify the seller"
    actor: "Us, with the seller's written authority to verify"
    document: "Registry and news checks, then a request to the seller's bank"
    receives: "The buyer receives a verification result. Nothing has been paid."
  - title: "Agree the contract"
    actor: "Buyer and seller"
    document: "Contract and delivery instruction"
    receives: "The seller receives a signed contract; the buyer holds a checked contract and instruction."
  - title: "Issue the payment instrument"
    actor: "Buyer's bank"
    document: "Documentary letter of credit or SBLC under UCP 600"
    receives: "The seller receives the instrument to check."
  - title: "Receive the performance bond"
    actor: "Seller's bank, after the instrument is checked"
    document: "Performance bond"
    receives: "The buyer receives security for performance."
  - title: "Shipment and documents"
    actor: "Seller"
    document: "Inspection certificates, bill of lading, commercial invoice"
    receives: "The banks receive the documents the instrument requires."
  - title: "Payment"
    actor: "Buyer's bank"
    document: "Payment against compliant documents"
    receives: "The seller is paid; the buyer receives the cargo documents."
---

Money moves after documents, not before. Two signs end a deal at the first step: a price that looks too good against the market, and any payment requested from the buyer before a deal exists, such as a deposit for certifying or legalising documents.
