audience: seller
commodity: []

steps:
  - title: "Check the request"
    actor: "Seller"
    document: "The buyer's ICPO or letter of intent"
    receives: "The buyer learns whether its request is current and addressed to the right company."
  - title: "Issue the offer"
    actor: "Seller"
    document: "Offer with its validity, often five banking days"
    receives: "The buyer receives an offer it can accept within the validity."
  - title: "Open the file for verification"
    actor: "Seller"
    document: "Corporate documents and written authority to verify with the seller's bank"
    receives: "The buyer's side can verify the seller beyond open sources."
  - title: "Sign the contract"
    actor: "Seller and buyer"
    document: "Contract naming the instrument, the performance bond and the documents for payment"
    receives: "The buyer receives a contract with a clear document route."
  - title: "Receive and check the instrument"
    actor: "Seller and its bank"
    document: "The buyer's letter of credit or SBLC"
    receives: "The buyer receives confirmation that the instrument is workable."
  - title: "Issue the performance bond"
    actor: "Seller's bank"
    document: "Performance bond, calculated on the instrument"
    receives: "The buyer receives security for performance."
  - title: "Load and prepare documents"
    actor: "Seller"
    document: "Inspection certificates, bill of lading, commercial invoice, as the instrument requires"
    receives: "The banks receive a presentation that complies with the instrument."
  - title: "Get paid"
    actor: "Seller's bank"
    document: "Presentation under the letter of credit"
    receives: "The buyer receives the cargo documents; the seller is paid."
---

A performance bond is calculated on the letter of credit. Asking for the bond before the instrument exists cannot work: there is nothing to calculate it from. The seller that keeps this order protects both sides.
