Offer Check is now available as a fixed-scope product. We review one supplier offer — the seller and its authority, the product and its origin, the Incoterms basis and the payment terms — and return a written verdict: real, fixable or walk away, with the reasons.

The scope and the turnaround are described on the [commodity deal structuring page](/services/deal-structuring/). The fee is agreed before work starts.
