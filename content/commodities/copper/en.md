We work on copper deals for buyers who want to deal with a seller they can verify, and for sellers who need a structure a buyer's bank will accept. The deal is organised under a mandate: the parties contract and pay each other directly.

We check who the seller is and how close it stands to the material, its ownership and the signatory's authority, and the parties against sanctions lists. The structure follows the same discipline as every commodity: a delivery basis agreed in the contract under Incoterms 2020, a letter of credit or SBLC under UCP 600, independent inspection, and money that moves after documents.
