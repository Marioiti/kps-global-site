We work on diesel deals for buyers who need a seller they can verify and a product that matches the agreed specification, and for sellers who need a structure a buyer's bank will accept. The deal is organised under a mandate: the parties contract and pay each other directly.

We check who the seller is and whether the product is under its control, its ownership and the signatory's authority, and the parties against sanctions lists. The structure follows the same discipline as every commodity: a delivery basis agreed in the contract under Incoterms 2020, a letter of credit or SBLC under UCP 600, independent inspection against the contract specification, and money that moves after documents.
